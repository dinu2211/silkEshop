Online-only store policy

Thank you for shopping online with Weave Made. We take pride in the quality, weave, and traditional craftsmanship of our premium dhotis. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help with a hassle-free, complimentary return process.

To start a return or ask for help, contact Customer Care on WhatsApp: 7418758951

01

Return & exchange window

Same-day / 24-hour dispatch constraint: To safeguard the value and integrity of our delicate traditional fabrics, the product must be returned via courier on the same day or within 24 hours of delivery. Requests initiated or packages handed over to the courier service outside this 24-hour timeline will not be accepted or processed.

02

Eligibility for returns & exchanges

To be eligible, the dhoti must satisfy all of the following conditions:

  • Unused & unwashed: It must not have been worn, ironed, altered, or laundered.
  • Original condition: It must be free from disturbed folds, stains, fragrance, body odors, or alterations such as customized border stitching or tailoring.
  • Packaging & tags: All original brand tags, tag attachments, inner linings, and packaging boxes must be completely intact.
  • Proof of purchase: Your digital order invoice or email confirmation number is required.
  • Unboxing video: For missing, damaged, or wrong items, an uninterrupted video recorded from the moment the outer courier parcel is opened is mandatory.
03

Non-returnable / final sale items

For hygiene and strict quality assurance, the following cannot be returned or exchanged:

  • Dhotis that have been worn, washed, customized, or altered.
  • Custom-dyed, personalized orders, or custom-sized traditional garments.
  • Items purchased under final clearance sales, unless verified as damaged on arrival.
04

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

  • Report a defective or incorrect product immediately, within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Contact Customer Care on WhatsApp at 7418758951 with your Order ID, clear descriptive photos, and the mandatory unboxing video.
  • Once approved, we will arrange a priority replacement or issue a full refund at no additional expense to you.
05

Complimentary return shipping & courier charges

  • Free returns: Weave Made will pay the return courier charges.
  • Pickup services: Once your timeline-compliant request is validated, we will schedule our courier partner to collect the package from your delivery address at no cost to you.
06

Refunds & processing time

  • Once your returned dhoti reaches our facility and passes physical quality-control checks, we will email you a confirmation with your refund status.
  • Approved refunds will be returned to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds will be made by bank transfer or UPI after we coordinate payment details with you on WhatsApp at 7418758951 .
07

How to start a return

  1. Within 24 hours of receiving the parcel, visit our online return page or message Customer Care on WhatsApp at 7418758951 with your Order Number.
  2. Carefully pack the dhoti in its original box with all tags securely attached.
  3. Hand the package to our designated reverse-courier representative on the scheduled day.
